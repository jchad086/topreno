Common questions

Before you call.

Do you only work with HVAC, roofing, and plumbing companies?

Those are our core trades, and it is deliberate. Working within a narrow set of trades means we already know your buying cycle, your seasonality, and what a good lead actually looks like. We do take on adjacent trades such as electrical, insulation, and general renovation when the fit is right.

How quickly will I see results?

Paid advertising can produce inbound calls within the first two weeks. Local search and Google Maps ranking typically take three to six months to compound. Websites and brand work usually launch inside four to six weeks. We set expectations for each channel during discovery so you are never guessing.

Am I locked into a long-term contract?

No. We work on month-to-month retainers after the initial engagement. If we are not earning our keep, you should be free to leave — and that keeps the pressure on us where it belongs.

Do I own my website and ad accounts?

Yes, completely. Your domain, website, Google Business Profile, and advertising accounts are created in your name and stay yours. There is no hostage-taking if the relationship ends.

What does “quote-ready lead” actually mean?

A homeowner or property manager who has a real job, is inside your service area, and is ready to have someone come out and price it. We tune targeting and forms to filter out tire-kickers and job seekers so your team spends its time quoting rather than qualifying.

Which areas of Ontario do you serve?

We work with contractors across Ontario, with dedicated support in Ottawa, Kingston, London, Kitchener–Waterloo, Barrie, Peterborough, and Cornwall. Everything we do is remote-friendly, so location is rarely a barrier. See all service areas.
