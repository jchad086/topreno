About us

We help blue collar businesses dominate their trade.

TOP Reno exists because good contractors keep losing work to worse contractors with better marketing. We are here to even that up.

The short version

Marketing and IT outsourcing, so you can focus on what matters.

Most owners we meet are the best technician in the company, the top salesperson, the dispatcher, and the bookkeeper. Something has to give — and it is usually the marketing, because it is the only one that does not scream at you when it gets ignored.

You should not have to become a marketer to keep your crews busy.

We take the whole digital side off your plate: the website, the advertising, the listings, the reviews, the reporting, and the tedious back-office workflows that quietly eat your week. You stay focused on the work, the crews, and the customers in front of you.

We are not a general agency that happens to have a contractor on the client list. Trades are all we do — specifically HVAC, roofers, and plumbers building trust with homeowners and local businesses across Ontario.

What you get working with us

Four things we hold ourselves to.

Focused expertise

We partner exclusively with Canadian HVAC, roofing, and plumbing companies. That narrowness is the whole point — we are not learning your industry on your budget.

Industry experience

Our team has roots in trades businesses and has helped scale them. We understand seasonality, call volume, crew capacity, and why you cannot just “turn on more leads” in July.

Technical fluency

We are fluent in the tools that power trades businesses — scheduling, dispatch, CRM, quoting, invoicing — and we can wire them together instead of adding another disconnected app.

Partnership mindset

Our success is defined by your business’s success. We work month-to-month, we report in plain numbers, and every account we create is in your name from day one.

Who we work with

Three trades. One playbook each.

Different emergencies, different seasons, different buying decisions. We treat them that way.

HVAC

Heating & cooling

Emergency no-heat calls in January, AC installs in July, and maintenance plans that carry you through the shoulder seasons. We build for all three cycles instead of only chasing the busy months.

Roofing

Roofing & exteriors

High-ticket, high-consideration, and heavily review-driven. Homeowners compare three quotes and pick the company that looks most credible. We make sure that is you.

Plumbing

Plumbing & drains

Urgent, local, and won or lost on the phone within minutes. Fast-loading pages, click-to-call everywhere, and a Maps listing that shows up when water is on the floor.
